If you've followed the streams or the Discord, you already know how I think about the market. This blog is where that thinking gets written down — so it's searchable, referenceable, and here long after a live stream scrolls off the feed.

## What you'll find here

The plan is simple: publish often, keep it real, and cover the stuff that actually moves the needle for a short-side small-cap trader.

- **Trade breakdowns** — setups that worked, setups that didn't, and why.
- **Weekly market recaps** — what ran, what faded, and what I'm watching next.
- **Written versions of the videos** — recaps of the YouTube content so you can read it, skim it, or find it later.
- **Process notes** — risk, sizing, and the discipline behind every entry.

## Why write trades down at all

Because the screen time is only half of it. The traders who improve are the ones who can look back at what they did and be honest about it. Writing forces that. Every post here is part of the same process I teach: no hype, no highlight reels — just how I actually trade, including the losses.

> Discipline creates a freer tomorrow.

New posts land weekly at a minimum. If you want the live version — the daily premarket work, the squawk, and the room — that's inside the community.
